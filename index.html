Munting Diwata

Choose Mela, Vera or Guita. A tiny diwata flies through the moonlit cliffs and waterfalls of Makiling, where moths and beetles are as big as she is. Shoot the glowing lilies to free dewdrops, keep hitting a dewdrop to change what's inside, then fly into it for a power-up.

Power-ups

Lilies that glow brightest hold a dewdrop. It starts gold; shoot it 5 times and the next power-up appears inside, in this order.

  • Gold: points500, then 1,000, 2,000… up to 10,000 for each gold dewdrop in a row. Let one escape and the count resets.
  • Multi-shotMore streams side by side, up to 3.
  • SpreadAdds angled shots, up to 2 pairs.
  • SpeedMoves faster with the keys, up to 3 levels.
  • ShieldTakes 3 hits for her.
  • LambanaA little fairy follows her path and shoots too. She can have two.

Who's in the forest

  • Moths, beetles and lamokMoths weave in rows, golden salagubang fly in threes and take two hits, ladybugs swoop from above or below, and mosquitoes hover, then dart at her.
  • SantelmoA floating ghost-fire that stops to shoot.
  • Duwende and kapreOn the ground, duwende throw pebbles from their glowing mounds, and a kapre in its tree puffs smoke rings. Seeds take them out; the kapre needs two. Some leave golden ginger worth 1,000 points.
  • Pamana ng mga BabaylanThe Babaylans' Legacy, a book on the title screen. The babaylan were the healers and storytellers of the old Philippines, often women, who kept the stories of the spirits. Every creature and spirit she meets gets a page: how to say its name, where its stories come from, what the lolos and lolas say, and one Filipino word to learn. Between zones and after a game over, an Alam mo ba? (Did you know?) card shares another fact.
  • The journeyThree zones: Gubat ng Makiling (Makiling Forest), Bayan sa Gabi (a town at night, where wakwak, aswang bats, the White Lady, nuno and the laughing bungisngis giant come out) and Dagat at Langit (sea and sky, where a sirena sings). Watch for the tikbalang in the forest: when it stops and glows, it is about to charge!
  • BossesThe Great Salagubang, then the Manananggal with its rings of dark feathers, then the Bakunawa itself: when a band of light flashes, it charges along it. Free the moon and the story ends.

If she's hit, she loses one level of each weapon and speed, and her lambana. She gets an extra life every 50,000 points.
